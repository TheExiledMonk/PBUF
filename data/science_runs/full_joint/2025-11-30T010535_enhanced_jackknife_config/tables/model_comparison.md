**Footnotes:**
1. Model: Cosmological model name (LCDM or PBUF)
2. N_params: Total number of parameters (fitted + derived)
3. N_fitted: Number of parameters fitted in the optimization
4. N_derived: Number of derived quantities computed from fitted parameters
5. AIC: Akaike Information Criterion = 2k + χ² (lower is better)
6. BIC: Bayesian Information Criterion = k·ln(n) + χ² (lower is better)
7. ΔAIC: Difference in AIC from baseline model (LCDM)
8. ΔBIC: Difference in BIC from baseline model (LCDM)
9. χ²: Total chi-squared for the model fit
10. χ²/ndf: Reduced chi-squared (χ² divided by degrees of freedom)
11. Evidence interpretation: ΔAIC/ΔBIC < 2 = strong, < 6 = moderate, < 10 = weak, ≥ 10 = no evidence
