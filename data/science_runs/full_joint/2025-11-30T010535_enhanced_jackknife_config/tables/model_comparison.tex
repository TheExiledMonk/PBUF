\begin{tablenotes}
\small
\item[1] Model: Cosmological model name (LCDM or PBUF)
\item[2] N_params: Total number of parameters (fitted + derived)
\item[3] N_fitted: Number of parameters fitted in the optimization
\item[4] N_derived: Number of derived quantities computed from fitted parameters
\item[5] AIC: Akaike Information Criterion = 2k + χ² (lower is better)
\item[6] BIC: Bayesian Information Criterion = k·ln(n) + χ² (lower is better)
\item[7] ΔAIC: Difference in AIC from baseline model (LCDM)
\item[8] ΔBIC: Difference in BIC from baseline model (LCDM)
\item[9] χ²: Total chi-squared for the model fit
\item[10] χ²/ndf: Reduced chi-squared (χ² divided by degrees of freedom)
\item[11] Evidence interpretation: ΔAIC/ΔBIC < 2 = strong, < 6 = moderate, < 10 = weak, ≥ 10 = no evidence
